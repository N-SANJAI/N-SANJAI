### 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Computer Engineering** student at the **National University of Singapore** who loves turning ideas into working software.

I build full-stack apps, automate things with Telegram bots, and create tech content on YouTube. Most of my projects start from a real annoyance - splitting bills, managing calendars, or ordering hawker food without queuing.

- 🔧&ensp;Comfortable in **Python**, **Java**, **TypeScript**
- 🤖&ensp;Big on **Telegram bots** & API integrations
- 📹&ensp;Running **[Tech NoLogic](https://www.youtube.com/@TechNoLogic_Sanjai)** on YouTube
- ⚡&ensp;Currently exploring **data analytics** & **automation**
